HBD Foundation (Happy Birthday Foundation) is a global celebration brand for birthdays, beginnings, milestones, anniversaries, achievements and memories.

Logo directions

Three symbols that avoid cakes and balloons. The spark is used throughout this guide.

A · HBD Heart

Letters held inside a heart: people, love and celebration.

B · HBD Spark

Bold wordmark with a spark: the moment something comes into existence. Recommended primary.

C · HBD Origin

A point expanding outward: everything begins somewhere. Works as app icon and pattern.

Voice and philosophy

Birth is the beginning. Life is the journey. Milestones are the memories. Celebration is how we remember that it mattered.

Mission: to celebrate the people, beginnings, milestones and moments that make life meaningful. Values: Joy, Recognition, Connection, Creativity, Memory, Inclusivity, Excellence.

Growth path: Celebrations, then Milestones, Beginnings, Marketplace, and finally HBD Global.
